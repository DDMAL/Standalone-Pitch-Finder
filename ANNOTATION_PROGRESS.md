`scripts/annotate_notecenters.py`) for the CNN-based pitch finder. Update by
re-running the checks below rather than editing this file by hand -- it's a
snapshot of `labels/human_annotated_stave_steps.json` files across pages,
not a source of truth itself.

A folio with 0 staff-line boxes has none in Gen's own JSON -- its stafflines
were generated by running `mothra/staff-finding` ourselves instead of
converting Gen's boxes.

"Heuristic unchanged / corrected" counts non-skipped labeled glyphs by
whether the saved step matches the heuristic's own pre-filled guess
(`accepted_heuristic` in the labels JSON) -- i.e. how often the annotator
had to actually correct something vs. just confirm it. "Heuristic accuracy"
is unchanged / (unchanged + corrected). Most pages cluster around 80-92%.
Two are clear outliers on the low end:
- `CantusMA1537 p.22` (2.6%) has no real Gen staff-line boxes at all -- its
  stafflines came from our own noisy self-run staff-finding (heavily
  fragmented, duplicate-prone), so this is expected.
- `Antiphonale officii Windeshemense I p.009` (57.9%) and
  `NZ-Wt MSR-03 065r` (62.1%) *do* have real Gen staff-line boxes, so the
  low accuracy isn't explained by missing stafflines the same way -- for
  Windeshemense the stave regrouping was fragmented (several staves ended
  up with only 1-3 of their 4 lines), which is a plausible partial
  explanation; 065r's cause hasn't been dug into yet.
